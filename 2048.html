How to Play 2048

1. Swipe or use arrow keys to move all tiles.

2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one.

3. Every valid move adds a new tile.

4. Reach the 2048 tile to win. Keep playing to beat your high score!

Game Tips

Keep your largest tile in a corner. Avoid moving in random directions and plan each move carefully.
